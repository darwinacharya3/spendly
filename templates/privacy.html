Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect the information you provide on the registration form: your full name, email address, and password.

When you use Spendly to track your spending, we collect the expense information you choose to enter, such as category, amount, date, and description.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your account information to create and manage your account and to let you sign in. We use the expense information you enter to display your records back to you, including summaries and breakdowns of your spending by category and time period.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense information is stored in Spendly's application database and is associated with your account. It remains accessible to you through the application for as long as your account exists.

4. Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for signing out of shared or public devices.

5. Third-Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts to display its typography. Loading these fonts may involve a request from your browser to Google's servers, which is subject to Google's own privacy practices. Spendly does not share your account or expense information with Google or any other third party.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and expense information for as long as your account remains active. If you request that your account be closed, we will remove or deactivate your account in line with our Terms and Conditions.

7. Your Rights

You can review and manage the expense records you have entered within the application. You may request that your account and associated information be deleted by discontinuing use of the Service and requesting account closure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of Spendly after any changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
